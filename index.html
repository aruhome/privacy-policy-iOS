Privacy Policy for AruHome (iOS)

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Smart Remote Labs (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides the AruHome iOS app (the “Service”). This policy explains how AruHome handles information. It applies to the App Store version of AruHome.

AruHome is built as a local-first, offline home inventory app. You do not create an account with us. Your home records stay on your device unless you export them yourself.

1. Local data you enter

You may store asset details, photos, receipts, documents, serial numbers, warranties, maintenance schedules, expenses, meter readings, vendor names, and similar household records.

This information is saved on your iPhone or iPad using Apple’s on-device storage (SwiftData / the device file system). We do not host your inventory on our servers. We cannot see, recover, or reset your records.

You are responsible for backups. Use the app’s backup/export feature if you want a copy off the device.

2. Permissions

AruHome asks for permissions only when you use a feature that needs them:

  • Camera — scan QR labels, appliance plates, receipts, or documents. Images are processed on the device.
  • Photo library — attach photos you choose. We do not browse your library in the background.
  • Notifications — optional reminders for maintenance due dates and expiring warranties.

You can deny or later revoke these in iOS Settings. The rest of the app still works.

3. Purchases

The app is free to download. Optional Homeowner Toolkit access is a one-time In-App Purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit.

We do not receive or store your Apple ID, credit card number, or billing address. Apple’s privacy policy applies to payment processing. Restore Purchases re-checks Apple’s purchase record on this device.

4. What we do not collect

The iOS app does not:

  • Create an account or sign you in
  • Upload your inventory, photos, or documents to our servers
  • Use advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or analytics SDKs
  • Sell or share personal information with data brokers
  • Track you across other companies’ apps or websites

On-device text recognition uses Apple’s Vision framework and does not upload images to us.

5. Links and exports you start

If you open a website link (for example our support site) or share a backup, CSV, or PDF, that action is started by you. We do not receive the file unless you email it to us for support.

Universal links for QR labels may open aruhome.github.io. That site does not receive your inventory contents.

6. Children’s privacy

AruHome is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has entered personal data, contact us and we will help you remove it from the device.

7. Security

Because records stay on your device, security also depends on your device passcode, Apple ID lock, and who can unlock the phone. No method of storage is perfectly secure. Uninstalling the app deletes local AruHome data on that device.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
